How the night runs

Your staff press one button. After that the game runs the evening.

  1. We set the night upYou get a link for the telly. Open it, and the room sees a code and a QR.
  2. Your customers scan and name their teamStraight into a browser. No sign-up, no download, no barrier.
  3. Press startThe game runs the draft, the reveal and the table on its own. Players decide when the next round begins.
  4. Call time when you're readyFour rounds is about fifty minutes. Ends on a winner, on the big screen, with the room watching.
